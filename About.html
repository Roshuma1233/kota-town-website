Our story

About Kota Town

Kota Town is a Johannesburg-based street-food brand celebrating South African kota culture. We aim to serve affordable, loaded kotas made with familiar ingredients and bold flavours.

Our mission

Our mission is to bring generous portions, satisfying flavour and a welcoming community feel to every meal. We serve students, workers, families and anyone who enjoys a good kota.

Our vision

We want Kota Town to grow into a favourite local food destination and a proud celebration of authentic kota culture.

What matters to us

  • Big, satisfying portions.
  • Freshly prepared food and flavourful combinations.
  • Friendly service and clear communication.
  • Celebrating South African street-food culture.
